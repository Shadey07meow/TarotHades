# Memoriam: Aspect of Fate

## Overview
`Memoriam: Aspect of Fate` is a top-down action roguelite based on the Major Arcana of the Classic Tarot–a symbolic journey of growth, loss, and transformation.  The world has been fractured into two opposing realities after the balance of the Arcana collapsed, scattering its power across the land.

In a world fractured in two, you play as The Fool, the first card of the Tarot and the beginning of every journey. With no memory, no homeland, and no true identity, The Fool awakens carrying only a staff  and the ability to wield fragments of the Arcana. To uncover the truth behind the shattered world, you must travel through four dangerous realms, each representing a stage of the Fool’s progression through the Tarot.

Every level is governed by three Major Arcana cards, whose themes shape the enemies, environments, and challenges within them. As you conquer each realm, you claim Minor Arcana cards that alter your abilities and define your playstyle for the remainder of the run. These powers stack and evolve, allowing every journey to unfold differently.
	
But the Arcana were never meant to be controlled by mortals. The closer The Fool comes to the end of the journey, the more the line between destiny and free will begins to disappear. At the end awaits The World, the final Arcana, the keeper of completion, cycles, and perfection, whose defeat may either restore balance… or break the cycle entirely.

## Installation & Execution Instructions
Here are the different ways you are able to compile game

### CLI (Command Line Interface)
- From the root directory
    
    `cd Memoriam & javac -d bin -sourcepath src src/MainGameDriver.java & java -cp bin MainGameDriver && cd ..` 
- From "The F4tes/Memoriam"
    
    `javac -d bin -sourcepath src src/MainGameDriver.java & java -cp bin MainGameDriver`

- Jar and run (From root directory)

    `jar cvfm Installer/Jar/Memoriam.jar Memoriam/manifest.txt -C Memoriam/bin . -C Memoriam assets`

### NOTE!!!

Must run the jar and exe file where there are
 - APPDATA folder
 - assets folder

### Installation
- Go to Installation/Windows folder and open `Memoriam.myp`
- During installation, you may or may not create a desktop shortcut (Select create a desktop shortcut for convience)
